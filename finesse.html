From job-shadowing to backtesting

Finesse Stock Broking Services

I was there to watch an analyst trade options. A few days in, I asked for real work and spent my evenings learning quant and algorithmic trading so I could do it.

I wrote Python scripts to backtest hedging strategies on five years of market data, calculated returns and maximum drawdown, compared each strategy across market conditions, and wrote up the risk versus return trade-offs for my mentor's portfolio discussions.

Leadership/FORTI Economics Society

Co-Founder & Curriculum Developer · Since 2025

An economics society with FORTI

With one co-founder and the Federation of Rajasthan Trade and Industry (FORTI), I built a society where students debate markets through real events.

I design every case: what the US takeover of Venezuelan oil assets meant for Chevron and ExxonMobil, how a Union Budget moves different asset classes, and how the Harshad Mehta scam reshaped Indian regulation. My favourite was an Iran crisis simulation where Iran, US and China delegations argued both the geopolitics and the hit to their own companies.

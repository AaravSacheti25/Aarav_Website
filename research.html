Research

Four papers, one theme: how better information leads to better financial decisions.

01 · Published · Oxford Journal of Student Scholarship

ESG mutual funds in India

I compared five years of Indian ESG fund data using Sharpe and Treynor ratios and t-tests, and tested best-in-class screening against negative exclusion.

  • ESG funds beat the benchmark, and exclusion beat best-in-class, but not by a statistically significant margin. That says a lot about how young India's ESG market still is.
  • Also submitted to the Indian Conference of Finance. Advised by Dr. Tapati Sarmah Choudhary.

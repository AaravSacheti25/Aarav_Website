Work/Shahar Saheli

02Co-Founder & Project LeadSince 2025

Shahar Saheli

A park that feels fine at noon can feel very different after sunset, and women make that call every day with almost no information. I wanted that information to come from the women who actually use these spaces.

I joined in Grade 9 as a researcher, interviewing 100+ women about street lighting. As project lead I moved the focus to parks. With 14 volunteers, I surveyed 600+ women about 9 of Jaipur's main parks at three times of day, scored each park on lighting, footfall, visibility and lived experience, and put the scores on a live map that updates with every visit.

Five women's organisations now use the map, and the Rajasthan Institute of Public Administration (RIPA) has recognised it. My next step is getting the findings to the people who can fix lighting, patrols and upkeep.

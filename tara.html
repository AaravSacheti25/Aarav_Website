Work/Tara Sansthan

Student Lead, Financial Audit & Welfare Monitoring

Tara Sansthan

Gauri Widow Help Scheme: financial assistance and disbursement monitoring

Tara Sansthan's Gauri Widow Help Scheme gives widows regular financial assistance. When a payment is missed, the woman waiting for it often has no easy way to flag it.

As student lead for financial audit and welfare monitoring, I reconciled 650+ widow-aid records against what had actually been paid, and built a payment tracker that flags missed payments.

Tara now uses the tracker for 800+ beneficiaries, so widows get their support on time.

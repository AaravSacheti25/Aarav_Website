Things I've built

Five projects I started or helped build. Each one tackles a problem where better information leads to better decisions.

01 · Founder & Fund Manager

EmpowerHer Impact Fund

I met a woman with a profitable saree-stitching business who still couldn't get a loan. She had customers and income, but no collateral or credit history. So I started a fund that judges businesses on real sales, real customers and real room to grow.

  • 7 of 50+ businesses screened and funded
  • ₹2L raised from companies
  • +17% average revenue growth

02 · Co-Founder & Project Lead

Shahar Saheli

A park that feels fine at noon can feel very different after sunset, and women make that call every day with almost no information. I wanted that information to come from the women who actually use these spaces.

  • 600+ women surveyed
  • 9 × 3 parks × times of day
  • 14 volunteers I led
